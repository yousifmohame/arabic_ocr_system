Replace this file in your existing project:
  src/arabic_doc_ai/engine.py

completed successfully and should be preserved.

  text_detection_model_name="PP-OCRv5_mobile_det"
  text_recognition_model_name="arabic_PP-OCRv5_mobile_rec"
